# TRILLION

The website that makes $1 trillion a month. It doesn't, and the page shows why with live, sourced math.

**Live:** https://prab2112.github.io/trillion/

- A $1,000,000,000,000 banknote that gets stamped DECLINED when you press the button
- A live meter: $1T ÷ 2,629,800 seconds in an average month = $380,257 a second
- The audit: $12T a year is 9.4% of world GDP and 16.7× Amazon's annual revenue
- A price × customers simulator, the moving-vs-keeping-money math, and the doubling plan ($1 × 2⁴⁰)

One self-contained `index.html`: no build step, no dependencies, no network requests. Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.
